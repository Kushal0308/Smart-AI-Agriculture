About Smart Irrigation Predictor

Traditional irrigation methods often rely on guesswork and fixed schedules, which can lead to water wastage, higher electricity consumption, and reduced crop productivity. The Smart Irrigation Predictor is designed to solve these challenges by using AI and machine learning to analyze environmental conditions and provide data-driven irrigation recommendations.

Key Features

  • Analyzes soil moisture, temperature, humidity, rainfall prediction, and crop type.
  • Predicts whether irrigation is required and suggests optimal timing and duration.
  • Helps farmers save water and reduce electricity usage from pumps.
  • Improves crop yield and supports sustainable agriculture practices.

Impact

By combining technology with farming, this system empowers farmers to make smarter decisions, conserve resources, and increase productivity. It also contributes to long-term sustainability by promoting efficient water management and reducing environmental impact.
